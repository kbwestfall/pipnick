.. _scripts:

Processing and Analysis Scripts
===============================

The data processing and analysis is performed via a sequence of scripts.  Most
scripts require the table of raw frames generated by the first step
(``pipnick_metadata``) and edited by the user.  The pages below describe the
purpose of each script, its algorithm, its inputs and outputs, and brief
troubleshooting notes.  The order they are listed is the expected order that
they should be performed; a minor caveat is that the photometry can be performed
without the astrometry.  Each script will provide warnings or errors if it is
missing information required to complete its purpose.

See :ref:`using` for descriptions of how to check their results and additional
known issues.
